Roadmap

Timeline & Milestones

2016

9 Years Old

I first became interested in programming when I was 9 years old. Having limited internet access at the time, I relied on borrowed books and video tutorials downloaded whenever an internet connection was available. During that period, I focused on understanding the inner workings of HTML and CSS, experimented with PHP, and used XAMPP as a local server environment to build plane ticket booking simulations and online store mockups.

2019

12 Years Old

After spending several years learning how to write clean code, organize logic, and build familiarity with programming, I ventured into game development using Roblox Studio. I learned how Lua works and how it differs from JavaScript, HTML, PHP, and CSS. Because Roblox Studio tutorials were very limited at the time, especially in Indonesian, I learned primarily through reverse engineering existing open-source scripts, deconstructing every keyword and function, understanding data flow, and figuring out how to design clean architectures from scratch.

2020

13 Years Old

At this point, I began sharing what I learned about Roblox Studio by creating YouTube tutorials. I wanted to ensure the knowledge I gained could benefit anyone looking to learn, especially given that Indonesian Roblox Studio tutorials were virtually nonexistent at the time. This motivated me to step up and create accessible learning content for the community.
